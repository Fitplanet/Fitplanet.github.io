The basics

The 3 macros in simple words

💪

Protein

Builds and repairs muscle. Think eggs, chicken, daal, chana, dahi and milk.

⚡

Carbs

Your fuel for training. Think roti, rice, oats, potatoes and fruit.

🥜

Fats

Hormones and health. Think eggs, nuts, a little ghee and desi oil.

Smart habits

Easy tips for better desi eating

🥣

Cut oil, not flavour

Use less oil in curries and avoid deep frying.

🍚

Pair daal with rice or roti

Together they give a more complete protein.

🥗

Add vegetables

Sabzi, salad and fruit give fibre, vitamins and minerals.

🍗

Choose grilled or boiled

Chicken tikka beats karahi when you want fewer calories.

⚖️

Watch portions

Rice, paratha and biryani add calories fast.

💧

Drink water

Sip through the day and limit sugary drinks.

Budget protein sources in Pakistan

  • Eggs, daal, chana and soya chunks are usually the cheapest per gram of protein.
  • Milk and dahi are affordable and easy to add to any meal.
  • Chicken is a good lean option when your budget allows.
