How to Get Healthy Nutrients from Desi Meals

You don't need expensive imported food. Daal, roti, chicken, eggs and dahi can cover your protein, carbs and fats on a student budget.

The 3 Macros in Simple Words

Protein

Builds and repairs muscle. Think eggs, chicken, daal, chana, dahi, milk.

Carbs

Your fuel for training. Think roti, rice, oats, potatoes, fruit.

Fats

Hormones and health. Think eggs, nuts, ghee in small amounts, desi oil.

Easy Tips for Better Desi Eating

  • Cut oil, not flavour: use less oil in curries and avoid deep frying.
  • Pair daal with rice or roti to get a more complete protein.
  • Add vegetables: sabzi, salad and fruit give fibre, vitamins and minerals.
  • Choose grilled or boiled over fried when possible, like chicken tikka over karahi.
  • Watch portions: rice, paratha and biryani add calories fast.
  • Drink water through the day and limit sugary drinks.

Budget Protein Sources in Pakistan

  • Eggs, daal, chana and soya chunks are usually the cheapest per gram of protein.
  • Milk and dahi are affordable and easy to add to any meal.
  • Chicken is a good lean option when your budget allows.
