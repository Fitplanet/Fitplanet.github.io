Progress rules for beginners

📖

Learn form first

Start with light weights and watch tutorials or ask a trainer.

📈

Progressive overload

When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add a little weight.

📝

Leave 1-2 reps in reserve

Do this on most sets, so you are not training to failure every time.

😴

Sleep 7-9 hours

Muscle grows while you rest.

🍗

Eat enough protein

Check your targets with the macro calculator and use the desi nutrition guide.

⏳

Be patient

Real changes take 8-12 weeks of consistency.

Frequently asked questions

How many days a week should a beginner go to the gym?

Three non-consecutive days a week is ideal for beginners, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday, with a different muscle group focus each day.

How long should a beginner workout take?

About 45 to 60 minutes including the warm-up.

Stop if you feel sharp pain and see a doctor if you have injuries or medical conditions.
