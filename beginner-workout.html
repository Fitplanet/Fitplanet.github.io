Progress Rules for Beginners

  1. Learn form first. Start with light weights and watch tutorials or ask a trainer.
  2. Progressive overload: when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add a little weight.
  3. Leave 1-2 reps in reserve on most sets, so you are not training to failure every time.
  4. Sleep 7-9 hours. Muscle grows while you rest.
  5. Eat enough protein. Check your targets with the macro calculator and use the desi nutrition guide.
  6. Be patient. Real changes take 8-12 weeks of consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days a week should a beginner go to the gym?

Three non-consecutive days of full body training, like Monday, Wednesday and Friday, is ideal.

How long should a beginner workout take?

About 45 to 60 minutes including the warm-up.

Stop if you feel sharp pain and see a doctor if you have injuries or medical conditions.
